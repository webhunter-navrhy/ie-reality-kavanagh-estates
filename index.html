Description

15 Westbury Woods, Enniscorthy, Co. Wexford

Kavanagh Estates are delighted to bring 15 Westbury Woods to the market. This well-presented three-bedroom semi-detached property extends to approximately 106 sq. m. and offers spacious and well-proportioned accommodation throughout. The property is ideally suited to first-time buyers, families or those looking for a comfortable home in a convenient location.

The ground floor comprises a welcoming entrance hall, a bright and spacious kitchen/dining area with ample storage and double doors opening directly onto a large paved patio area, making it ideal for entertaining and outdoor dining. The living room features an open fire, creating a warm and comfortable living space, while a downstairs bathroom completes the ground floor accommodation.

Upstairs, there are three generously proportioned double bedrooms, with the master bedroom benefiting from an ensuite. A main family bathroom completes the first floor.

Outside, the property benefits from an enclosed rear garden with a large paved patio area and garden shed, providing an excellent private outdoor space.

The property is serviced by oil-fired central heating and offers well-proportioned accommodation in a popular residential development.

Westbury Woods is conveniently located on the outskirts of Enniscorthy town, within easy reach of a wide range of amenities including shops, schools, supermarkets, cafés, restaurants and leisure facilities. Enniscorthy town centre is easily accessible, while the N11/M11 is also within convenient driving distance, providing excellent connectivity to Wexford, Gorey and Dublin.

Viewing is highly recommended to appreciate all that this property has to offer.
